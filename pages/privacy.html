HypeOtter Uploader - Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026.

HypeOtter Uploader is used only by the HypeOtter channel on its own accounts. It does not collect, sell or share personal data from anyone else.

What it stores: the access tokens for HypeOtter's own accounts, kept in a private, access-controlled repository, plus a record of which HypeOtter videos were posted where. It does not store viewer data, messages, contacts or other users' information.

How it's used: only to upload HypeOtter's own videos to HypeOtter's own accounts.

Removal: access can be revoked at any time in the connected platform's app settings, and stored tokens are deleted on request.
